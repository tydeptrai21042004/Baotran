11H20 TIMEOUT EXPORT POLICY
============================

Applied to every training bash cell in this package.

1. The wall-clock guard starts from the beginning of the cell and fires at 11 h 20 min (40,800 s).
2. When it fires, the cell first sends TERM and then KILL (if needed) to active training process groups.
3. The cell immediately writes three exports in /kaggle/working:
   - <JOB>_11H20_CURRENT_DONE_results.zip
     Explicit timeout export containing all non-checkpoint experimental result files, logs, configs, completed final-seed results, and completed LR-candidate metadata available at the guard.
   - <JOB>_DONE_ONLY_results.zip
     Contains only methods that have complete final test summaries for every requested seed.
   - <JOB>_results.zip
     Method-level progress/state metadata used by the same cell on a later Kaggle session.
4. The timeout ZIP contains 11H20_CURRENT_DONE_MANIFEST.json, which lists complete methods, complete final seed runs, completed LR candidates, and failed/non-finite LR candidates.
5. Model checkpoint files (.pth/.pt/.ckpt) are intentionally omitted from the CURRENT_DONE and normal result ZIPs to keep timeout export fast and small.
6. The cell exits with code 124 after the timeout export is flushed to disk.
7. If the cell finishes before 11 h 20 min, the normal DONE_ONLY and result ZIPs are still created; the special 11H20 timeout ZIP is only needed when the guard fires.

This preserves the manuscript protocol: one shared validation-selected LR per method, with non-finite LR candidates excluded globally rather than allowing per-seed LR cherry-picking.
